# Privacy Policy — Little Express

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Little Express (Android app ID com.himanshu.littleexpress) is a single-player idle game. This policy explains what data the game and the services inside it use.

## What the game itself stores

- Your game progress (coins, upgrades, quests, settings) is saved only on your device.
- The game has no account system of its own and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.
- The game does not sell personal data.

## Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded ads: an ad plays only when you tap a button to watch one for a reward. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares with Google:

- your device's IP address (which can give an approximate location),
- interactions with ads and the app (for example app launches, taps and video views),
- diagnostic information (for example app performance),
- device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

This data is encrypted in transit. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads). Google's use of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services": https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Google Play Games (optional, if enabled)

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, your progress can be saved to your Google account using Play Games Services. Signing in is optional and handled by Google.

## In-app purchases (if enabled)

Gem packs are sold through Google Play Billing. Payments are handled entirely by Google; the game never sees your card or payment details.

## Children

Little Express is not directed at children under 13.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes all progress stored on your device. For data held by Google (ads, Play Games), use your Google account settings.
