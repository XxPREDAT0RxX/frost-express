# Privacy Policy — Frost Express

Last updated: October 2026

Frost Express does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

- All game progress is saved only on your device (local app storage).
- The app does not ask for any permissions, accounts or contact details.
- The app currently shows no ads and has no analytics or tracking.

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, your game progress is stored in your Google account using Google Play Games Services (Saved Games), under Google's privacy policy. Signing in is optional.

If rewarded ads or purchases are added in a future update, this policy will be updated first and will describe the data those services use.
